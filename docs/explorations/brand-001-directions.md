# BRAND-001 hero directions

Both live explorations show the same unbranded white T-shirt, headline, supporting copy, and size-selection action. They use the existing local SVG product illustration and system fonts, with serif and sans-serif pairings chosen for Cotton Fold?s gentle, effortless, youthful voice; no external font or reference artwork is embedded.

## Modernist

- **Reference:** Josef Müller-Brockmann, _Der Film_, 1960, offset lithograph, Museum of Modern Art collection: <https://www.moma.org/collection/works/4882>.
- **Observed choices:** oversized cropped geometric title; large areas of black negative space; compact red exhibition information.
- **Exploration choices:** a quiet editorial two-column grid; Georgia headline paired with a clean sans-serif utility face; warm paper tones, rounded action, and an arched product field.
- **Intended effect (hypothesis):** help the everyday wearer scan the message and find size selection quickly.
- **Risk:** the restrained editorial treatment may feel too formal or mature for a youthful visitor.
- **Live preview:** [`modernist.html`](../../public/explorations/modernist.html).

## Postmodernist

- **Reference:** April Greiman, _Your Turn, My Turn_, 1983, offset lithograph and screenprint, Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum: <https://www.cooperhewitt.org/2017/08/30/26363/>.
- **Observed choices:** offset red/cyan lettering; overlapping photographic/halftone texture; diagonal geometric forms.
- **Exploration choices:** a pastel collage field, playful offset headline shadow, tilted product card, and rounded sans-serif details; the copy remains on a readable ground.
- **Intended effect (hypothesis):** make the plain tee feel more expressive to a visitor seeking an easy everyday choice.
- **Risk:** the layered pastel composition may feel busy beside the effortless voice or distract from size selection.
- **Live preview:** [`postmodernist.html`](../../public/explorations/postmodernist.html).

## Shared content

- **Headline:** “Plain white, made for every day” (frequent-wear implication not verified).
- **Supporting copy:** “A simple white tee for easy days and quick mornings”.
- **Action:** “Choose your size,” linking to S, M, L, and XL demo radio choices.
- **Product facts:** plain white, unbranded T-shirt; no price, material, fit, durability, or quality claims added.
