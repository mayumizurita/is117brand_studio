Size guide

Compare a T-shirt you already like with the garment measurements when those are available.

  1. Lay a similar T-shirt flat on a level surface.
  2. Measure across the chest from underarm seam to underarm seam, then from the top of the shoulder to the bottom hem.
  3. Compare those measurements with the product size chart.

This demo has no garment measurements, so its S, M, L, and XL choices cannot confirm fit or recommend a size.
