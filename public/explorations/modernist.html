Modernist direction

Reference: Josef Müller-Brockmann, Der Film, 1960, offset lithograph, MoMA collection. View the work at MoMA.

Design choices: A fixed two-column grid separates message from product; a large plain sans-serif headline leads into one high-contrast action; restrained ink, paper, and red details echo the reference’s disciplined hierarchy and economical color use.

Intended effect (hypothesis): The calm typography and clear sequence may make an everyday choice feel gentle and easy to scan.

Risk: The stark contrast and rigid grid may feel formal beside Cotton Fold’s gentle voice.

Design study only. The layout is an original interpretation, not a reproduction.
