Postmodernist direction

Reference: April Greiman, Your Turn, My Turn, 1983, offset lithograph and screenprint, Cooper Hewitt, Smithsonian Design Museum. View the work at Cooper Hewitt.

Design choices: Offset color shadows, rotated labels, and overlapping geometric planes reinterpret the reference’s layered lettering, halftone texture, and angled shapes. The headline stays on a clear ground, and the size action keeps a solid outline for legibility.

Intended effect (hypothesis): A youthful collage may make the plain tee feel more expressive while retaining a soft mood.

Risk: The layered pastel composition may feel busy beside the effortless voice or distract from size selection.

Design study only. The layout is an original interpretation, not a reproduction.
